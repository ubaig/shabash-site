AI Family Assistant

Your family's always-on smart coach — proactive nudges, natural language commands, and real-time celebrations. Coming soon to Shabash.

Natural Language Commands

Type or speak in plain language — "Mark Clean Yard as done for Ahmed" or "Can Sara afford a reward?" — and the assistant takes care of the rest.

Proactive Nudges

The assistant monitors your family's activity and surfaces timely reminders, deadline alerts, and approval queue warnings before things slip through the cracks.

Real-time Celebrations

When a child hits 100 stars, unlocks a badge, or can finally afford a reward, the assistant celebrates the moment instantly — no nagging required.

Privacy-First AI

The entire model runs on-device inside the Shabash server. Your family's conversations and data never leave your account — zero cloud AI, zero data sharing.

Your Family Shouldn’t Need a Manager

The toughest part of running a chore system isn’t setting it up — it’s keeping the momentum going. Kids forget. Parents get busy. Approvals pile up. Streaks break quietly.

The Shabash AI Assistant is designed to handle all of that in the background, so you don’t have to.

Two Modes, One Goal

Reactive — Ask it anything, anytime:

  • “What’s pending approval?”
  • “Give Ahmed 5 bonus stars”
  • “How did the kids do this week?”

The assistant understands your family’s unique names, chore titles, and rewards. It uses semantic understanding to handle fuzzy phrasing — so “yard cleaning” and “Clean Yard” both work.

Proactive — It tells you things you didn’t know to ask:

  • “3 chores have been waiting for your approval for over a day”
  • “You’re 2 stars away from unlocking Star Collector!”
  • “Ahmed hasn’t completed any chores in 3 days”
  • “Everyone can now afford Pizza Night — want to redeem together?”
  • “You’ve finished Clean Room 10 times in a row — awesome streak!”

Voice-Enabled

Tap the microphone and speak naturally. The assistant listens, interprets, and responds — hands-free, from anywhere in the app.

Smart, Not Noisy

The assistant is designed to reduce alert fatigue, not add to it. Each nudge type fires at most once per day per situation. You’ll hear about what matters — and only when it matters.

Built for Shared Windows Hosting

Unlike other AI features that require expensive cloud APIs, Shabash’s assistant runs entirely on-server using a lightweight ONNX model. Fast, private, and free to use — no per-request API cost passed on to families.
