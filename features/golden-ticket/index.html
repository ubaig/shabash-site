Golden Ticket

Golden Ticket Invites

Shabash grows by word of mouth — existing families hand-pick who joins. Your Golden Ticket is how you bring the people you trust into your community.

5 Personal Invites

Every family that completes onboarding receives a unique 8-character Golden Ticket code with 5 invite slots to share with friends.

Visual Usage Tracking

See exactly how many of your invites have been claimed with a clear slot indicator — gold means available, grey means used.

One-tap Sharing

Share your code or a direct referral link via native mobile share, copy-to-clipboard, or your favourite social app.

Referral Dashboard

Track who accepted your invitations, when they joined, and celebrate growing the Shabash family together.

Invite-Only, by Design

Shabash doesn’t have an open sign-up. Every family that joins was personally invited by one that is already thriving on the platform. This keeps the community tight-knit, purposeful, and free from spam.

How You Earn Your Ticket

Golden Tickets are awarded automatically once you finish the core setup:

  1. Verify your email address.
  2. Add at least one child to your family.
  3. Create your first chore and first reward.

The moment those three steps are done, your dashboard reveals your unique code — ready to share.

Sharing Made Simple

Your Golden Ticket shows up as a beautiful card on your parent dashboard. From there you can:

  • Copy the code to paste wherever is convenient.
  • Share a link that pre-fills the code for the new parent.
  • Use the native share sheet on iOS or Android to send it directly through WhatsApp, Messages, or email.

Each share shows a preview: “You’ve been invited to Shabash! Join my family on Shabash — use my Golden Ticket to skip the waitlist.”

No Ticket? Join the Waitlist

Don’t have a code yet? Drop your email on our waitlist and we’ll reach out as soon as a spot opens up. You’ll be at the front of the queue when the next wave of invitations goes out.

Quality over quantity — that’s the Shabash way.
